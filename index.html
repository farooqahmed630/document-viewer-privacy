Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

Farooq Ahmed ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Document Viewer mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

Documents and Files

Document Viewer allows you to open, view, and save documents on your device.

Your documents and files are processed and stored locally on your device. We do not upload your documents to our own servers, and we do not operate a server that stores your documents.

We do not access or collect the contents of your personal documents for our own purposes.

Device and Advertising Information

Document Viewer uses the HUAWEI Ads SDK to display advertisements.

The HUAWEI Ads SDK may process certain information such as device identifiers, device information, app information, network information, IP address, advertising interaction information, and other information described in Huawei's applicable privacy documentation.

2. How We Use Information

Information handled by the App is used to:

  • Provide document viewing functionality.
  • Allow users to open and save documents locally on their devices.
  • Display advertisements through the HUAWEI Ads SDK.
  • Support advertising measurement, attribution, fraud prevention, and related advertising services.

We do not use your documents for advertising or sell the contents of your documents.

3. Third-Party Services

Document Viewer uses the following third-party SDK:

SDK: HUAWEI Ads SDK
Provider: Huawei
Purpose: Advertising, ad measurement, attribution, fraud prevention, and related advertising services.

For more information about how Huawei handles information through its advertising services, please review Huawei's applicable privacy documentation.

4. Data Storage

Documents opened or saved using Document Viewer remain on your device unless you independently choose to share, move, delete, or otherwise transfer them using your device's functionality.

We do not maintain a server for storing your documents.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share the contents of your documents with third parties.

The HUAWEI Ads SDK is integrated into the App to provide advertising services and may process information as described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. Because documents are stored locally on your device, the security of those files also depends on the security settings and protection of your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Document Viewer is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Choices

You can manage permissions granted to Document Viewer through your device settings.

You may also manage available advertising and privacy settings provided by your device or Huawei services.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.
